# Paper Trading Architecture

The `Backtesting_Engine` is designed using a generalized Event-Driven Architecture. This means the core logic for evaluating indicators, matching rules, and dispatching orders is completely agnostic to whether the system is running a historical backtest or a live paper trade.

## High-Level Pipeline

1. **Feed Layer**: Converts external data into standard `MarketEvent` (OHLC) objects.
2. **Strategy Engine**: Calculates indicators and evaluates AST logic on the `MarketEvent`.
3. **Execution Engine**: Simulates the fill of orders generated by the Strategy Engine.

## Historical vs Paper Trading

To switch from Backtesting to Paper Trading, we only swap the first and third layers:

### Backtesting
*   **Feed:** `HistoricalReplayFeed` (reads from CSV/DB and loops instantly).
*   **Execution:** `ExecutionEngine` (simulator that instantly fills orders against historical data with math-based slippage).

### Paper Trading
*   **Feed:** `LiveKiteFeed` (connects to Zerodha WebSocket. Buffers ticks in real-time, aggregates them into OHLC, and yields a `MarketEvent` only when the timeframe minute closes).
*   **Execution:** `PaperExecutionEngine` (queues pending orders, monitors incoming live ticks, and simulates realistic slippage before marking the order as FILLED).

## Data Aggregation (LiveKiteFeed)

The most important concept in `LiveKiteFeed` is **Candle Aggregation**. 
The Strategy Engine cannot run on raw ticks because indicator math (like RSI) requires closed periods to prevent repainting.
Therefore, `LiveKiteFeed` acts as a middleman:
1. Receives raw ticks (LTP) continuously.
2. Tracks the highest high and lowest low over the course of the minute.
3. At the end of the minute, it yields one clean `MarketEvent` candle.
4. The Strategy Engine processes this exactly like a historical row.

## API Integration

To start a paper trade session, send a `POST` request to `/api/v1/run_paper_trade` with a payload including your `kite_api_key` and `kite_access_token`. 
The engine will run in a background thread and expose its live PnL state via `GET /api/v1/paper_trade/{session_id}/status`.
